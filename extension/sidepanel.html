One-time setup so this app may use Ollama:

                        
    
                            
  1. Make sure the Ollama app is installed and running.
    2. 
                            
  2. Open the Terminal app
                                (press the ⌘ Command key + Spacebar, type “Terminal”, then press Enter).
                            
    3. 
                            
  3. 
                                Copy the line below, paste it into Terminal, and press Enter:
                                
    
                                    launchctl setenv OLLAMA_ORIGINS "chrome-extension://…"
                                    
                                
    
                            
    4. 
                            
  4. Quit Ollama (click its icon in the top menu bar → Quit) and open it again.
    5. 
                            
  5. Click Refresh models below, choose your model, then Connect.
